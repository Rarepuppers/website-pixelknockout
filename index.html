    GAME RULES

    Rules

    Points

    • Glory Points are virtual internet points only.
    • New players receive a free signup grant.
    • Players receive a free event grant for each active card.
    • Limited live-event bonuses may appear during configured event windows.

    Predictions

    • Pick a fighter and choose how many free points to risk.
    • Predictions lock when the event starts.
    • Once locked, picks cannot be edited.
    • Difficulty multipliers are frozen at the moment picks are locked.

    Settlement

    • Correct picks return stake multiplied by the locked payout.
    • Missed picks lose the staked points.
    • Cancelled or voided bouts refund the stake and do not count as wins or losses.
    • Results are settled after official fight outcomes are known.

    Seasons & Awards

    • Leaderboards reset each calendar year.
    • Event performance can earn virtual badges, belts, and shrine items.
    • One shrine item can be displayed beside your player name.
    • All awards are cosmetic and have zero monetary value.

    No purchases or cash-out

    PKO has no paid entry, no cash-out, no transferable points, and no prize of monetary or material value. Points, badges, belts, and shrine items cannot be bought, sold, traded, transferred, or redeemed.

    How the season works

    Every calendar year is a fresh scoreboard. Points, ranks, and seasonal standings reset on January 1. Historical badges, belts, and shrine items remain visible as cosmetic profile history.

    Leaderboard

    Season rank is based on total Glory Points after grants, predictions, live bonuses, refunds, and settled outcomes.

    Event Awards

    Single-event performance can award gold, silver, bronze, copper, and iron badges for the top five event scores.

    Belts

    Division belts are virtual awards for standout event performance by weight class. They are cosmetic only.

    Shrine

    Your shrine stores earned virtual items. You can choose one item to display beside your player name.

    COMMUNITY RULES

    Fair Play

    One player, one account

    Do not create duplicate accounts to farm signup grants, event grants, bonuses, rankings, or shrine items.

    No automation

    Scripts, bots, browser automation, or API abuse that claims points or submits predictions for you are not allowed.

    Keep names clean

    Display names that are hateful, explicit, harassing, impersonating, or designed to bypass filters may be reset or removed.

    Report issues

    If a fight is cancelled, a score looks wrong, or a bonus window behaves incorrectly, contact the developer before trying to exploit it.

    No real-world value

    Do not offer, request, sell, trade, transfer, or assign real-world value to Glory Points, rankings, badges, belts, shrine items, or accounts.
