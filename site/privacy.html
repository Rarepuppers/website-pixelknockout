Privacy Policy

Plain-language summary of what we collect and why. This is a starting template, not legal advice.

1. What we collect

  • Account info: your email address and a display name you choose, provided when you sign in (via Google sign-in or an email magic link).
  • Game data: your Glory Points balance, predictions, leaderboard rank, and any virtual belts/badges earned.
  • Basic technical data: standard logs (e.g., IP, browser type) used to keep the service running and secure.

We do not collect payment-card details — donations are handled entirely by Stripe, which has its own privacy policy.

2. How we use it

  • To run your account, track Glory Points, and show leaderboards and profiles.
  • To prevent cheating, abuse, and duplicate accounts.
  • To communicate essential service messages (e.g., the magic-link sign-in email).

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Who we share it with

Service providers that make the game work: Supabase (authentication and database) and Stripe (donations). Each processes data under its own policy. We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Public information

Your display name, leaderboard rank, points, and earned belts/badges are public within the game (that's the whole point — bragging rights). Don't use a display name you want kept private. Your email is never shown publicly.

5. Your choices

You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us. Deleting your account removes your profile, points, and predictions.

6. Children

PKO is intended for users 18 and older and is not directed to children.

7. Contact

Questions? Reach the developer at hello@pixelknockout.com (replace with your real contact before launch).

See also our Terms of Service.
