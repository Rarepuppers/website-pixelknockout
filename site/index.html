    How PKO works

    🎟️

    Free points, every event

    Every UFC/MMA event, everyone gets the same 1,000 Glory Points. No one can buy more — there's no store.

    🥊

    Predict the winner

    We drop our pixel parody fighters into the real matchups and spend points predicting who wins. We only use the real fight's result to settle.

    📈

    Odds = difficulty

    Real Vegas odds set the multiplier. Call an upset, win more points. Play it safe, win less.

    🏆

    Belts & bragging rights

    Climb the season leaderboard. Top 5 wear cosmetic belts. Board resets every year (2026, 2027, …).

    This is not gambling

    • ✅ Glory Points have zero monetary value.
    • 🚫 You cannot buy points — money never enters the game.
    • 🚫 You cannot sell, trade, transfer, or cash out points.
    • 🎁 There are no prizes — just a number on a scoreboard.
    • 🔁 Points are free with every event and reset each year.

    Gambling requires paying to play and a prize of value. PKO has neither — it's the same as a free office prediction pool or Reddit karma.
